How Read Forward Works

Getting a book is as easy as sending a message — no credit card, no subscription.

1

List Your Books

Add books sitting on your shelf that you're happy to lend out to fellow readers.

2

Request a Book

Browse books near you and send a borrow request. The owner gets notified instantly.

3

Exchange & Read

Coordinate delivery or pickup. Read the book, then send it forward to the next reader.

4

Earn Litcoins

Every book you lend earns you Litcoins — use them to borrow more books for free.
